What we do for your farm, garden, and nurseries

The services we provide to keep your land healthy and productive:

Soil Health Check

We test your soil and hand you a simple plan to fix it with our products — no confusing reports.

Organic Compost & Biofertilizers

We supply biofertilizer, biochar, compost, vermicompost, and green manure that feed your soil instead of just dressing it.

Natural Pest & Disease Control

Neem biopesticides, trap crops, and companion planting — practical ways to protect your crops without harsh chemicals.

Tailor-Made Farm Equipment

We make innovative farm equipment designed specifically to suit the needs of Arid Land Agriculture.

Seed & Variety Advice

Help choosing traditional, locally adapted varieties that handle your climate and still give you a market-worthy harvest.

Crop Rotation & Mixed Cropping

Simple rotation and intercropping layouts that keep soil alive, break pest cycles, and improve your yield.

Water & Irrigation Equipment

Practical ways to use less water — mulching, drip irrigation, fertigation, sprinklers, and watering schedules matched to your crop's real needs.

Field Visits & Farmer Training

We come to your field, walk it with you, and answer questions on the spot. Group training sessions available for interested clients.

Organic Certification Support

Paperwork, record-keeping, and inspection prep handled step by step, so going certified doesn't feel like a maze.
